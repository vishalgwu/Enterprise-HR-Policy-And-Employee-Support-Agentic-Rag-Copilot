# Attendance and Working Hours

## Standard Working Week
The standard working week is 40 hours across five days, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours
are 10:00 to 16:00 in your registered time zone; start and finish times outside those hours are
flexible provided deliverables and meeting commitments are met. Your registered time zone is set in
Workday and changes only with HR approval.

## Recording Attendance
Salaried employees do not clock in and out. Hourly and contract staff record hours daily in Workday
and submit timesheets by 18:00 each Friday. A timesheet not submitted within seven days is escalated
to the reporting manager. Managers approve timesheets by 12:00 the following Monday so payroll can
close on schedule.

## Unplanned Absence
Notify your manager before 10:00 local time on the first day of any unplanned absence, by direct
message or phone. Email alone is not sufficient. Absences of three or more consecutive working days
require a medical note, filed through the HR Service Desk. Three unnotified absences in a rolling
12-month period trigger a formal attendance review.

## Lateness
Arriving after 10:00 without prior notice is recorded as lateness. Four or more instances in a
rolling quarter prompt a documented conversation with your manager. Lateness caused by public
transport disruption, severe weather, or a caring emergency is excused when reported the same day.

## Overtime
Overtime applies to hourly employees only and must be approved in advance by the reporting manager.
Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate, and at 2.0 times on public
holidays. Salaried employees do not accrue overtime; sustained excess hours should be raised with
the manager and are addressed through workload planning, not payment.

## Public Holidays
Employees receive the public holidays gazetted for their country of employment, published each
December for the following year. Where a public holiday falls on a weekend, the substitute day
follows local statutory practice. Public holidays are separate from PTO and are never deducted from
the PTO balance.
