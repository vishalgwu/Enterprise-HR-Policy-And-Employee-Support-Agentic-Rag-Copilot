# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Most individual-contributor and management roles are eligible for hybrid work. Roles requiring
physical presence, such as facilities, hardware labs, reception, and onsite security, are designated
onsite-only in the job requisition. Eligibility is determined by role, not by tenure or performance.

## Standard Hybrid Schedule
Hybrid employees work from a company office a minimum of three days per week, including Tuesday and
Wednesday, which are designated all-hands collaboration days. The third day is chosen by the team.
Teams record their anchor days in the team charter so scheduling is predictable.

## Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote arrangements require director-level approval and are reviewed every 12 months. Fully
remote employees must be located in a country where the company has a registered legal entity.
Relocation to another country is not permitted without prior written approval from HR and Legal, as
it creates tax and employment-law exposure. Notify HR at least 60 days before any planned relocation.

## Equipment and Home Office Stipend
Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750, claimable within the
first 90 days of eligibility, and an annual $200 refresh allowance. The company provides a laptop
and, on request, a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Company equipment remains company property
and must be returned within 10 business days of the last working day.

## Working Hours and Availability
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your registered time zone. Outside core hours,
schedules are flexible provided deliverables and meeting commitments are met. Employees must not be
expected to attend recurring meetings outside their core hours.
