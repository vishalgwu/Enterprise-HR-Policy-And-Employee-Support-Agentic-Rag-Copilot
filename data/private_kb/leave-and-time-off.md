# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.67 days
per completed month of service. New joiners begin accruing from their first full month. PTO requests
must be submitted in Workday at least 10 business days in advance for absences longer than three
consecutive days. Your manager must approve or decline within five business days.

A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire
on 31 March. Unused days beyond the carry-over cap are forfeited and are not paid out, except where
local law requires payout. On termination, accrued but unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not accrue and does
not carry over. A medical note is required for absences of three or more consecutive working days.
Notify your manager before 10:00 local time on the first day of absence. Sick leave may also be used
to care for an immediate family member.

## Parental Leave
Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing and adoptive parents receive 8
weeks of fully paid leave, which must be taken within 12 months of birth or placement. Parental
leave runs concurrently with any statutory entitlement. Notify HR at least 30 days before the
intended start date. Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to a phased return of up
to four weeks at 80% schedule on full pay.

## Bereavement and Jury Duty
Five paid days for the loss of an immediate family member; two paid days for extended family. Jury
duty is paid in full for the duration of service. Forward the summons to hr-ops@company.internal.
