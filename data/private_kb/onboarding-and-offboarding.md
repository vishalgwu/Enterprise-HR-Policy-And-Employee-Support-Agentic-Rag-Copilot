# Onboarding and Offboarding

## Before Day One
Signed offer, right-to-work documents, and background-check consent must be returned at least five
business days before the start date. The hiring manager raises equipment and access requests through
the HR Service Desk at the same time, so a laptop and accounts are ready on day one. A named buddy
is assigned to every new joiner before they start.

## First Week
Day one covers IT setup, security and compliance training, and benefits enrolment. New joiners have
30 days from their start date to make benefit elections; elections are backdated to day one, when
coverage begins. Payroll registration must be completed in Workday within the first three business
days to guarantee inclusion in the first pay cycle.

## Probation
The probation period is three months for individual contributors and six months for managers.
Managers hold documented check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days. Probation may be extended once, by up to
three months, with written notice before the original end date. Confirmation of employment is
recorded in Workday; probation does not lapse into confirmation automatically.

## Resignation and Notice
Resignation is submitted in writing to the reporting manager and through Workday. Notice is four
weeks for individual contributors and eight weeks for directors and above, unless the employment
contract states otherwise. Notice may be shortened only by written mutual agreement. Accrued but
unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck.

## Exit Process
An exit interview is offered to every leaver and is voluntary; responses are reported in aggregate
and are never attributed to an individual. Company equipment must be returned within 10 business
days of the last working day. All system access is revoked at 18:00 on the last working day.
Confidentiality and intellectual-property obligations survive the end of employment.

## Final Pay and References
Final pay, including any PTO payout, is issued in the payroll cycle following the last working day.
The company provides factual references only, confirming job title, dates of employment, and, with
written consent, final salary. Managers must not provide personal references on company letterhead.
Request an employment-verification letter through the HR Service Desk; it is issued within five
business days.
