# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Submission Deadline
Expenses must be submitted in Expensify within 60 days of the transaction date. Claims submitted
after 60 days require VP approval and may be declined. Claims older than 120 days are not
reimbursable under any circumstance.

## Travel
Book flights through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under six
hours; premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or more. Business class requires VP
approval. Hotel spend is capped at $250 per night in tier-1 cities and $160 per night elsewhere.
Rental cars require prior manager approval; ride-share is preferred for trips under 50 km.

## Meals
Meal per diem is $75 per full travel day, covering all meals and incidentals. Itemized receipts are
required for any single expense of $25 or more. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a documented
client-entertainment expense with the client names recorded in the claim.

## Non-Reimbursable
Personal entertainment, traffic and parking fines, airline seat upgrades, lost personal property,
in-room minibar charges, and loyalty-program fees are not reimbursable. Home internet is not
reimbursable; it is considered covered by the home office stipend.

## Approval and Payment
Claims under $500 are approved by your direct manager. Claims of $500 or more require second-level
approval. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle, typically within 15 business days.
