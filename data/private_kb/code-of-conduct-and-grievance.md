# Code of Conduct and Grievance Procedure

## Expected Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, candidates, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are prohibited and are grounds for
disciplinary action up to and including termination. This applies to conduct in offices, at company
events, in customer settings, and on company communication channels including Slack and email.

## Conflicts of Interest
Disclose any outside employment, board seat, consulting arrangement, or financial interest in a
competitor, customer, or supplier. Romantic relationships between an employee and someone in their
reporting line must be disclosed to HR so reporting lines can be adjusted. Disclosure is confidential
and is not itself a disciplinary matter; failure to disclose is.

## Raising a Concern
Concerns may be raised with your manager, any HR business partner, or through the anonymous
whistleblower line at ethics.company.internal. Anonymous reports are investigated with the same rigor
as named reports. You are not required to raise a concern with your manager first if your manager is
the subject of the concern.

## Investigation Process
HR acknowledges every grievance within two business days and assigns an investigator who is not in
the reporting line of any party involved. The investigator interviews the complainant, the respondent,
and any witnesses. Target resolution is 20 business days; complex cases may extend, and the
complainant is updated every 10 business days. Both parties receive a written outcome.

## Non-Retaliation
Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, or who participates in an
investigation, is treated as a separate and serious violation, independent of whether the original
grievance is upheld.
