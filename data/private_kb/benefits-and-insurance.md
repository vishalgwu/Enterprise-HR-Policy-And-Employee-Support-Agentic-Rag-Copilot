# Benefits and Insurance

## Health Coverage
Medical, dental, and vision coverage begins on your first day of employment; there is no waiting
period. The company pays 100% of the employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums. Two plan tiers
are offered: a PPO and a high-deductible plan paired with an HSA. The company contributes $1,200
annually to the HSA for individual coverage and $2,400 for family coverage.

## Enrollment Windows
Open enrollment runs for two weeks each November, effective 1 January. Outside open enrollment, you
may change elections only after a qualifying life event, such as marriage, divorce, birth or
adoption, or loss of other coverage, and you must file the change within 30 days of the event.

## Retirement
The company matches 100% of 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base salary, and 50% of the next 2%.
Match vests immediately. Contribution changes take effect the following payroll cycle.

## Life and Disability
Basic life insurance at two times base salary is provided at no cost. Short-term disability pays 70%
of base salary for up to 12 weeks; long-term disability pays 60% thereafter. Employees may purchase
supplemental life coverage during open enrollment.

## Learning and Wellness
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for courses, conferences, and certifications,
approved by their manager. A $60 monthly wellness reimbursement covers gym memberships, fitness apps,
and mental-health subscriptions. Twelve counselling sessions per year are available at no cost
through the Employee Assistance Program.
