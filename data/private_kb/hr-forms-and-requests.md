# HR Forms and Requests

## Where to Raise a Request
Workday handles anything tied to your employment record: leave requests, personal and bank details,
timesheets, resignation, and benefit elections. The HR Service Desk handles everything that needs a
person to review it: letters, documentation, medical notes, corrections, and case work. Expensify
handles expense claims only. Requests sent by direct message or email are not tracked and will be
redirected.

## Common Forms
| Request | Where | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Leave request | Workday | Manager decision within 5 business days |
| Timesheet | Workday | Weekly, by 18:00 Friday |
| Employment-verification letter | HR Service Desk | 5 business days |
| Salary or visa-support letter | HR Service Desk | 5 business days |
| Change of legal name or marital status | HR Service Desk, with documents | 5 business days |
| Change of address or emergency contact | Workday | Immediate |
| Bank detail change | Workday | 10 business days before pay date |
| Medical note for absence | HR Service Desk | Acknowledged in 2 business days |
| Home office stipend claim | Expensify | Next payroll cycle |
| Expense claim | Expensify | Within 15 business days of approval |
| Grievance | HR Service Desk, marked confidential | Acknowledged in 3 business days |
| Subject access request | HR Service Desk | 30 calendar days |
| Flexible or remote working request | HR Service Desk, with manager endorsement | 20 business days |

## Priority and Escalation
Standard requests are answered within five business days. Requests marked urgent — a visa deadline,
a pay error, or a safeguarding concern — are triaged within one business day. If a request has had
no response past its stated turnaround, reply on the existing ticket to escalate rather than opening
a second one; duplicate tickets reset the queue position.

## Confidentiality
Grievances, whistleblowing reports, medical documentation, and subject access requests are visible
only to the assigned HR case owner. They do not appear to the reporting manager and are not part of
the general employee record.

## What HR Cannot Action
HR cannot backdate a leave request beyond the current pay period, change a performance rating after
the review cycle closes, release another employee's personal data, or give legal, tax, or
immigration advice. Requests of that kind are declined with a referral to the right team.
