# Payroll and Compensation

## Pay Schedule
Salaried employees are paid monthly on the 25th. Where the 25th falls on a weekend or public
holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day. Hourly employees are paid twice monthly, on
the 10th and the 25th, covering the period ending five business days before each pay date.

## Payslips
Payslips are published in Workday two business days before each pay date and remain available for
seven years. Payslips are never sent by email. A payslip query should be raised through the HR
Service Desk within 60 days of the pay date so it can be corrected in the current tax year.

## Pay Components
Gross pay comprises base salary, any approved overtime, shift allowances, and eligible bonus
payments. Statutory deductions, pension contributions, and elected benefit premiums are itemised
separately. Approved expense claims are paid through payroll in the next cycle, typically within 15
business days of approval, and are shown as a separate non-taxable line.

## Salary Review
Salaries are reviewed annually, effective 1 April, based on performance, role scope, and market
benchmarking. Review outcomes are communicated by the reporting manager in March. Off-cycle
adjustments are permitted only for promotion, a substantial change in role scope, or a documented
market correction, and require director and HR approval.

## Bonus
Employees in bonus-eligible roles have a target bonus expressed as a percentage of base salary,
stated in the offer letter. Payment depends on both company performance and individual performance
and is made with April payroll. Employees who join part-way through the performance year receive a
pro-rated award. Employees who have resigned before the payment date are not eligible.

## Bank Details and Corrections
Update bank details in Workday at least ten business days before a pay date; changes made later take
effect the following cycle. HR and Payroll will never request bank details by email, chat, or phone.
An underpayment confirmed by Payroll is corrected by off-cycle payment within five business days. An
overpayment is recovered by an agreed repayment schedule, never by a single unannounced deduction.
